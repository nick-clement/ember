What it does

A small macOS menu bar app for Hive heating. Check the temperature, change the target, boost for an hour, switch modes, see your weekly schedule, the outside temperature and the last 24 hours, without opening the Hive app. I just wanted something simple that you could open in a click instead of fumbling through the official app or the dreadful MacOS Home app. It's private: Ember runs on your Mac, signs in with your Hive login and talks only to Hive, plus GitHub to check for updates. No analytics, no accounts of its own.

Download
  1. Get the zip from the latest release, under Assets.
  2. Unzip it and drag Ember into Applications.
  3. Open it, then click Ember's temperature box in the menu bar and sign in with your Hive account.

Ember isn't notarized yet, so the first time macOS may refuse to open it. Go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway. After that Ember offers new versions itself.

Privacy

Your login stays in your Mac's Keychain and goes only to Hive. Ember talks to Hive, plus GitHub to look for updates. No analytics, no accounts, no permissions asked. The source is open, so you can check.

Ember is not made by or affiliated with Hive or Centrica. It uses Hive's unofficial API, so it can stop working if Hive changes something. Made by · Source
